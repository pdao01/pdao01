## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm an electrical engineering student who likes building hardware end to end: choosing components, drawing the schematic, laying out the board, hand-soldering it, and debugging it on the bench with a scope and a logic analyzer. I'm most at home where hardware meets firmware, from STM32 and ESP32 boards to FPGA and VLSI designs.

My focus is embedded systems and hardware design, with a growing interest in chip design, computer architecture, and photonics. In the Tufts Nanophotonics lab I work on optical setups and simulation. Outside the lab, I lead the electrical team for Tufts Robotics' Pacbot and teach Embedded Systems and Digital Electronics, helping 70+ students debug STM32 and FPGA hardware. My Economics minor keeps me curious about the business side of engineering, and I'm happiest building a good Excel model.
