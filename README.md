## Featured Projects

### 🔌 Solar + Battery Data Acquisition Board (Black Pearl Technology)
Designed a 4-layer, 2.5" round STM32 board in Altium Designer for a hazardous-environment (C1D1) product: MPPT solar charging, gas-gauge BMS, IMU and temperature sensing, 4 analog inputs plus RS485, LoRaWAN and WiFi/BLE, and a dual Hall-sensor magnetic wake mechanism. Handled component sourcing and BOM (102 components), schematics, layout, hand assembly, and bring-up. A 2-board redesign fit the enclosure's air-volume limit and lowered power consumption.
`Altium` `STM32` `Power design` `RF layout` `Bring-up`

### 🤖 Pacbot: Tufts Robotics
Electrical Team Lead since Sep 2025. Tuned the PID loop controlling the robot's motors, and design and debug the electrical system.
`PID control` `Motor control` `PCB` `Raspberry Pi`

### 🔬 Nanophotonics Research
Replaced a manual Digital Micromirror Device (DMD) reconfiguration with a Python algorithm, cutting reconfiguration time to seconds and improving measurement repeatability. Optimized photonic integrated circuit mode-coupler dimensions through iterative simulation (transmission efficiency 65% → 90%), and built an optical imaging setup to measure beam patterns from neural photonic probes.
`Python` `Photonic simulation` `Optical setup` `Lab instrumentation`

### 🚗 Robot Car
ESP32-based robot with encoder-feedback motor control (L293DNE H-bridge), color-sensor lane following, and distance sensing, written in C++ with a clean .h/.cpp structure.
`ESP32` `C++` `Control loops` `Sensors`

### 📡 IR Distance Sensor
Distance sensor built from a 940 nm IR LED and a silicon PIN photodiode, read directly by the ESP32's ADC.
`ESP32` `Analog front end` `Bench testing`

### 🔋 Battery & BMS Firmware (Viettel AI)
Implemented UART and CAN communication between a PCB and a battery pack, read and configured battery status registers, and worked with ROS messaging.
`UART` `CAN` `ROS` `Firmware`
